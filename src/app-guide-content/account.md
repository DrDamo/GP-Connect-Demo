# App Guide: Account

The Account page is reached via your name in the top-right of the header, and holds your personal settings.

## Personal details

Your full name, job title, date of birth, and address. These are visible to other members of your organisation.

## Organisation

Only an organisation admin can rename the organisation — everyone else sees this field disabled with an explanatory note.

## Security

Change your account password here. New passwords must be at least 8 characters and are checked for a match before saving.

## Billing

Billing isn't active yet — this section is a placeholder for a future paid plan, shown as "Free plan" with a disabled "Upgrade — coming soon" button.

## Onboarding & Help

This section lists each guided tour in the app, whether you've completed it, and a button to restart it. There's also a button to reset dismissed info-hint tooltips, and a link to open this App Guide.
